Greenhouse equipment from Angers to Marrakech

Angers → road → Montoir-de-Bretagne (Nantes Saint-Nazaire Port) → conventional sea freight → Casablanca → road → Marrakech

The case

Serres de l'Authion, a manufacturer of horticultural greenhouse structures based in Angers (49), has sold a consignment of steel frames and irrigation modules to Atlas Horti SARL, a distributor located in Marrakech (Morocco).

The goods are packed in 14 wooden crates and shipped as conventional (break-bulk) cargo from Montoir-de-Bretagne to Casablanca.

Gross weight7 200 kg
Volume16,8 m³
Exchange rate1 EUR = 10,85 MAD

Your task

  1. Work out the chargeable weight (T) for the main sea transport: in conventional shipping, 1 m³ = 1 T. The volume converted into weight is the fictitious weight; the carrier charges the greater of the actual weight and the fictitious weight. All other weight-based steps (handlings) are charged on the actual weight.
  2. Drag each step number and each step into the right row of the quotation sheet, then drag each Incoterm onto its subtotal row. Some chips do not belong in the sheet.
  3. Calculate every amount. Steps in France are in EUR; steps in Morocco are in MAD. Convert the CIF price into MAD, then carry on in MAD.
  4. Round every result to the cent. Use French number format (e.g. 1 234,56).
